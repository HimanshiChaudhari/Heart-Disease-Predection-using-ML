How It Works

A four-step visual process ensuring 100% mathematical consistency from input to prediction.

01

Enter Health Information

Input patient age, biological sex, resting blood pressure, cholesterol, resting ECG, and exercise stress data.

02

Data Processing

Numerical features are mean-imputed and scaled. Categorical features are mode-imputed and ordinal encoded.

03

Machine Learning Analysis

The preprocessed feature vector is evaluated by the serialized pipeline (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, etc.).

Feature Schema & Encoding Definitions

Feature Column Type Handling & Imputation Clinical Meaning & Observed Values
age Numerical Mean Imputed • Standard Scaled Patient age in years (28 - 77)
gender Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Biological sex: Female (0), Male (1)
chest pain type Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Asymptomatic, Atypical Angina, Non-Anginal, Typical Angina
resting bps Numerical Mean Imputed • Standard Scaled Resting blood pressure in mmHg (admission vitals)
cholestrol Numerical Mean Imputed • Standard Scaled Serum cholesterol in mg/dL
fasting blood sugar Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Blood sugar > 120 mg/dL: FALSE (0), TRUE (1)
restecg result Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Normal (0), ST-T Abnormality (1), LV Hypertrophy (2)
mx heart rate achieved Numerical Mean Imputed • Standard Scaled Maximum heart rate achieved during exercise stress
exang Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Exercise induced angina: FALSE (0), TRUE (1)
oldpeak Numerical Mean Imputed • Standard Scaled ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest (mm)
slope Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Peak exercise ST slope: Upsloping, Flat, Downsloping
major vessels visible(ca) Numerical Mean Imputed • Standard Scaled Number of major coronary vessels (0-3) colored by fluoroscopy
thal Categorical Mode Imputed • Ordinal Encoded Thallium heart scan: Normal, Fixed Defect, Reversible Defect
target Target Variable Binarized (target > 0) 0 = No Significant Disease | 1 = Presence of Heart Disease
